How to capture YouTube sound

Open the video in a separate Chrome tab. Click Start, select the Chrome Tab category, select the video tab, and check Share tab audio. Then play the video. Stop when your clip is finished.

Your recording stays in this browser and is saved directly to your computer. The site receives no audio, video or screen contents. Recording stops if you close this tab. Some protected content may appear black or silent.
