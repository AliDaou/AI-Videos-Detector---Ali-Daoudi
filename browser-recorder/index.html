How to capture YouTube sound

Open the YouTube video in a separate Chrome tab. Press Start, choose Chrome Tab (not Window/Entire Screen), select the actual playing video tab, and enable Share tab audio. Check the moving preview and frame counter before recording the entire video. Stop to finalize.

Your recording never uploads to this website. Chrome grants access only to the tab you select. Seekable WebM and MP4 files are finalized locally on your device. Screen sharing stops if you close the recorder. If Chrome does not capture actual video frames, remuxing cannot recreate them.

Old recording won't seek?

Select the broken WebM or MP4 file. The recorder will attempt to rebuild its duration and seek index entirely in your browser, leaving the original untouched. This can fix an unseekable timeline, but it cannot restore missing video frames.
